Featured Landmark Project

Upgrading of Wa – Bulenga – Yaala Road (KM 37.0 – 74.0)

A key trunk road corridor under the Ministry of Roads and Highways and the Ghana Highway Authority (GHA), funded by the Government of Ghana under The Big Push Project - National Infrastructure Development Project.

Contractor: Messers Astee Company Limited P.O. Box AT 244, Achimota, Accra
Supervising Engineer: Regional Highway Director Upper West Region, Ghana
Scope: 37 Kilometers (KM 37.0 - 74.0) Earthworks, Drainage & Paving
Funding Source: Government of Ghana Under The Big Push Program

This critical corridor connects major farming and trading towns across the Upper West Region to regional hubs and cross-border trade networks. The project scope includes extensive earthmoving, subgrade improvement, sub-base gravel spreading, crushed aggregate base compaction, double bituminous surface dressing, reinforced concrete box culverts, and high-visibility road safety markers.

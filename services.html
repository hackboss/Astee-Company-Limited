Civil Engineering • Highways

Road Construction & Highway Upgrading

Astee Company Limited is accredited to construct major arterial trunk highways, urban corridors, bypasses, and feeder roads throughout Ghana. We deploy advanced paving trains, automated motor graders, and compaction equipment to adhere strictly to Ghana Highway Authority (GHA) standards.

Technical Capabilities Include:

  • • Subgrade stabilization & deep cut/fill
  • • Crushed rock base & soil-cement base
  • • Asphaltic concrete wearing course
  • • Double surface dressing (seal coats)
  • • Road alignment surveying & geometrics
  • • Thermoplastic road line marking & cat-eyes

Hydraulic Infrastructure

Drainage Systems, Box Culverts & Earthworks

Ghana's tropical rain patterns necessitate robust stormwater hydraulics to protect pavements and prevent urban flooding. Astee Co. Ltd engineers reinforced concrete box culverts, outfall drains, retaining walls, and erosion mitigation solutions.

Hydraulic Solutions:

  • • Single & multi-cell reinforced box culverts
  • • Precast & in-situ reinforced trapezoidal drains
  • • Gabion mattresses & stone pitching revetments
  • • Subsurface perforated collector pipes
  • • Bridge abutments & wing walls
  • • River channel desilting & stormwater outfalls

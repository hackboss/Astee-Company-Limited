Who We Are

A Proven Force in Civil Engineering & Contracting

Astee Company Limited was incorporated in Ghana with a clear mission: to solve complex infrastructural bottlenecks by delivering world-class road construction, civil engineering, building construction, and logistics services.

Under the banner of our core motto — "We Build Visions" — we serve as trusted partners to central government bodies including the Ministry of Roads and Highways, the Ghana Highway Authority (GHA), municipal assemblies, corporate organizations, and private developers.

Our team comprises veteran project managers, chartered civil engineers, geotechnical experts, quantity surveyors, and heavy plant operators who bring rigorous technical standards to every square meter of earthwork and structural concrete.

"From our headquarters in Achimota, Accra, to our regional site installations in the Upper West, Northern, and Southern corridors, Astee Co. Ltd guarantees execution with speed, safety, and durability."

Safety First

Zero-Compromise Health, Safety & Environment Policy

At Astee Company Limited, human life and environmental stewardship always come first. Operating heavy earthmoving plant and high-traffic road sites demands zero tolerance for safety breaches.

  • Full PPE Adherence: Mandatory high-visibility apparel, steel-toe boots, protective helmets, and respiratory gear for all site personnel.
  • Traffic Control & Diversions: Comprehensive road signage, reflective bollards, and certified flagmen during active highway resurfacing.
  • Environmental Restoration: Strict erosion management, safe disposal of bitumen emulsions, and ecological restoration of quarry/borrow pits.

Certified Compliance Standards

Full compliance with Ghana Highway Authority HSE Directives, Ministry of Roads and Highways Guidelines, and Environmental Protection Agency (EPA) protocols.

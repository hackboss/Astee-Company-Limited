Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions regarding tendering, plant lease, and national deployment.

Does Astee Company Limited execute contracts outside Accra?

Yes! While our corporate headquarters are located in Achimota, Accra, we maintain full nationwide operational capacity. As demonstrated by our landmark 37km Wa-Bulenga-Yaala Road project in the Upper West Region, our site teams and mobile plant can deploy to any of Ghana's 16 administrative regions.

What certifications and classifications does Astee hold?

Astee Company Limited is fully registered in the Republic of Ghana as General Contractors and Civil Engineers, holding certified eligibility with the Ministry of Roads and Highways, Ghana Highway Authority (GHA), and Public Procurement Authority (PPA) standards.

Can we hire your heavy earthmoving machinery for short or long-term lease?

Yes. We provide plant hire services including hydraulic excavators, motor graders, vibratory rollers, bulldozers, and tipper trucks, complete with certified operators and on-site mechanical support.

How quickly can your engineering department prepare a tender proposal?

For standard bills of quantities and road rehabilitation inquiries, we typically provide preliminary feasibility assessments and quotations within 24 to 48 hours. For comprehensive FIDIC/GHA tenders, our quantity surveying and project management office prepares complete technical and financial submissions according to specified statutory deadlines.
